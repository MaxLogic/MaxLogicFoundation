# Nia Marrowglass

## Status

Nia is being built like a PC-level important NPC.

Confirmed choices so far.

## Role

Important NPC, not a Wicked One PC.

Human warlock, Veyr Marrowglass's natural daughter, secret betrayer of Marrowglass Tower, protected key to the Black Cradle.

## Calling

Warlock.

## Core ability

**Sorcery**.

## Sorcery path

**Illusion**.

Nia's magic should focus on hiding, misdirection, false images, altered perception, softened sounds, concealed routes, false calm, and survival through making the world look slightly wrong.

She is not primarily a blaster.

## Starting Warlock ability

**Counterspell**.

On a success when resisting magical effects, Nia may choose one of the Counterspell benefits:

- absorb the spell and clear 1 stress,
- establish a quirk about the caster's power,
- reflect the spell at the caster.

## Normal actions

- **Invoke 3** — primary magic action, Illusion spells, wards, and defensive sorcery.
- **Trick 2** — lies, misdirection, covering guilt, social survival.
- **Scan 2** — reading notes, ruins, people, ritual clues, and Marrowglass procedures.
- **Skulk 1** — hiding, sneaking, staying alive.
- **Banter 1** — human conversation, calming minions, and small diplomacy.

## Gear defense

**Defense against magic**.

Nia's warded cloak, ritual scars, counterspell training, and built-in Mind Shield let her avoid or deflect magical consequences once per cycle when the defense applies.

## Supply

- **Father's cipher key** — a small Marrowglass code-wheel, used to decode Veyr's notes, lab labels, old ward instructions, and partial ritual logs.
- **Black thread and chalk** — used to mark illusion anchors, silent lines, ward boundaries, and false seams in walls or floors.
- **Small mirror charm** — used for misdirection, checking glamours, catching reflected glyphs, and detecting when an illusion is looking back.

## Dark impulse

**Paranoid** — Nia assumes any safety is temporary and every kindness has a cost.

This usually manifests quietly: checking exits, hiding useful facts until she knows who needs them, sleeping lightly, flinching from locked rooms, and preparing excuses before anyone asks questions.

## Revelry

**Occult**.

Nia lives it up by disappearing into forbidden notes, ward diagrams, sealed correspondence, half-burned spellbooks, and small private rituals. Her revelry is not loud. It is the kind of quiet that leaves candlewax, blood-specks, and three new lies by morning.

## Current design notes

Counterspell fits Nia because she survived Veyr, Marrowglass Tower, the Black Cradle, and hostile magic by being prepared rather than by being physically strong.

It also supports the idea that her Mind Shield is not just passive protection, but part of a broader defensive magical discipline.

Nia's action spread makes her useful but not physically dominant. She is strongest when dealing with illusion, wards, secrets, misdirection, Marrowglass procedures, and dangerous magical systems.

Her paranoia should make her cautious and secretive, not openly villainous. She should remain useful, wounded, competent, and slightly tragic.
